Getting Started

What is LumaSleep?

LumaSleep helps you sleep better with calming ambient sounds, AI-generated soundscapes, smart sleep tracking, and bedtime reminders. Browse the Sounds tab to explore categories like rain, nature, white noise, and more. Tap any sound to start playing instantly.

How do I create an account?

When you first open LumaSleep, you'll be prompted to sign in. You can create an account using your email address, Google account, or Apple ID. All your preferences, playlists, and sleep data will sync to your account.

Sounds & Playback

How many sounds are available?

LumaSleep has over 70 curated ambient sounds across 8 categories: Rain, Nature, Ocean, Fireplace, White Noise, Melody, City, and ASMR. Free users have access to a selection of sounds, while premium subscribers can access the full library.

Can I swap sound variants?

Yes! Many sounds have multiple variants. Tap the swap icon on a sound card to browse alternative versions. Your preferred variant is remembered for each sound.

How does the sleep timer work?

Tap the timer icon in the now-playing screen to set a countdown. When the timer expires, playback gently stops. Choose from preset durations or set a custom time.

AI Sound Generation

How does AI sound generation work?

Go to the Create tab and describe the sound you'd like to hear — for example, "gentle rain on a cabin roof with distant thunder." Our AI will generate a unique soundscape based on your description. Generated sounds are saved locally and can be replayed anytime.

Are there limits on AI generation?

Premium subscribers can generate up to 50 AI sounds per month. Each generated sound can be up to 30 seconds long. Your generated sounds are saved to your device for unlimited playback.

Sleep Tracking

How do I connect sleep tracking?

Go to Settings and tap "Sleep Tracker." On iOS, LumaSleep connects to Apple Health. On Android, it connects to Health Connect. You'll be asked to grant permission to read your sleep data. LumaSleep never writes to your health data — it only reads sleep information.

What does the sleep score mean?

Your daily sleep score (0–100) is calculated from multiple factors: sleep duration (35%), sleep efficiency (25%), deep sleep percentage (20%), REM sleep percentage (15%), and wake disruptions (5%). A score of 80+ indicates excellent sleep quality.

Is LumaSleep a medical device?

No. LumaSleep is a wellness app, not a medical device. Sleep scores, stage breakdowns, and insights are for informational purposes only and should not replace professional medical advice. If you have concerns about your sleep health, please consult a healthcare professional.

Bedtime Reminders

How do I set up bedtime reminders?

Go to Settings and tap "Bedtime Reminder." Choose your preferred bedtime and enable the reminder. LumaSleep will send a gentle notification every night at your chosen time to help you build a consistent sleep routine.

Subscription & Billing

What's included in premium?

Premium unlocks: all 70+ sounds, AI sound generation (up to 50/month), full sleep analytics and insights, saved playlists, offline playback, and an ad-free experience.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime through your device's subscription settings. On iOS: Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions. Your premium access continues until the end of your current billing period.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you've reinstalled the app or switched devices, open LumaSleep and go to Settings > "Restore Purchases." Your subscription will be automatically detected through your App Store or Play Store account.

Troubleshooting

Sounds aren't playing

Make sure your device isn't on silent mode and the volume is turned up. Check your internet connection — sounds are streamed from our servers. If the issue persists, try closing and reopening the app, or check that LumaSleep has permission to use your network connection.

Sleep data isn't showing up

Ensure you've granted LumaSleep permission to read sleep data in your device's health settings. On iOS, check Settings > Health > Data Access > LumaSleep. Sleep data requires you to wear a compatible device (Apple Watch, Fitbit, etc.) or use a sleep tracking app that writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

AI generation failed

AI sound generation requires a stable internet connection. If generation fails, check your connection and try again. If the problem continues, try a simpler or shorter prompt description. Contact us if the issue persists.
